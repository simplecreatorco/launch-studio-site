Important reminders

Focus on only one thing at a time through the songs in a row. On your breathing songs, just focus on breathing correctly. Nothing else. This is how it becomes autopilot.

Your song choices can be flip-flopped between sections that match. A fast breathing song can become your fast mouth song on a different day.
