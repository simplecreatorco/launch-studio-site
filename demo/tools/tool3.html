Live Performance Quiz

What do
they see?

"90% of people listen with their eyes."

If you can sing every note right, but your face and your body are saying something different from the lyric, it confuses your audience. They won't know why they don't like it. They just don't.

We can build the whole house. We can build the entire plane. But if we don't work on this, you will never get off the ground.

How to take this

Best Way: Videotape yourself singing half of a slow song. Then videotape yourself singing half of a fast song. Watch them back, then answer.

Next Best Way: Stand in front of a mirror. Sing 30 seconds of a slow song, then 30 seconds of a fast song. Then answer.

#1 RULE: Answer what your body actually does, not what you want it to do. If you've never thought about it, say so. That answer counts too.

Why this matters

If you sing with all that emotion and your body does nothing, it causes confusion for the viewer. Most of our habits up there come from our insecurities. Nobody told you how to do this, so it's not your fault. Once you have the tools, it's yours to use.

Builder notes for Natalie and Rebecca (not customer-facing)

Scoring: 15 questions, 0 to 3 points each (45 max). The score is scaled so a perfect quiz shows 87. "I don't know" is always 0 points and is flagged as a blind spot [P4-045] [P4-046].

There is always something to work on. Anything under 3 points shows up in "What to work on." If a singer has fewer than 3 items, the tool adds "go deeper" items from the areas they did well in, so everyone gets pointed to the masterclass.

The common thread: any body area they fully nailed shows as "in line with how I teach it," followed by the related masterclass piece. That's the "since our styles are similar" pitch.
