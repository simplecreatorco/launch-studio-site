      Builder notes for Natalie and Rebecca (not customer-facing)

      How the free response works in V1: the singer types in their own words. The tool scans for about 400 phrases and maps them to 24 sample trouble spots. The singer confirms or adjusts on the next screen. It feels custom, and every answer lands on a mapped problem Natalie controls.

      In the real build: Claude replaces the phrase scan. It reads the singer's words and matches them to Natalie's full trouble spot list, so it catches anything phrased in a way we didn't predict. The confirm screen stays, so every result is still one of her mapped problems.

      What is placeholder: the 24 trouble spots, their explanations and the exercise pairings are Claude's first draft from the product mapping transcript and Tricks of the Trade. Natalie's exhaustive list replaces them [P3-032] [P3-033]. Her old client files with coach notes are the starting data [P3-102].

      Segmenting: strongest and weakest ability, stage comfort (1 to 10) and goal route singers to Vocal Agility, Live Performance (quiz first), Vocal Health + Maintenance, Vocal Dynamics and the marked-up library, and Artist Development (coming later).

      Safety line: anyone who says singing hurts, or has been hoarse for weeks, gets a "see a doctor" note at the top. Natalie to confirm the wording [verify].
